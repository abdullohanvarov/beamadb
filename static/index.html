How to Pair with QR Code

  1. 1
    Connect to Same Wi-Fi

    Ensure both this computer and your Android device are on the exact same local Wi-Fi network.

  2. 2
    Enable Wireless Debugging

    Open Settings → Developer options → toggle on Wireless debugging.

  3. 3
    Tap "Pair device with QR code"

    Tap inside Wireless Debugging and select Pair device with QR code.

  4. 4
    Scan & Done!

    Scan this QR code. BeamADB automatically handles TLS security authentication and connects your phone wirelessly.

💡 Tip: Developer options not showing?

Go to Settings → About Phone → tap Build Number 7 times until you see "You are now a developer!".
